IN THE COURT OF THE ADDITIONAL CHIEF METROPOLITAN MAGISTRATE
MUMBAI

Case No. 1245/2023

BETWEEN:
M/s Horizon Traders Pvt. Ltd. (Complainant)
AND
Mr. Rajesh Kumar Sharma (Accused)

COMPLAINT UNDER SECTION 138 OF THE NEGOTIABLE INSTRUMENTS ACT, 1881

FACTS OF THE CASE:

The complainant M/s Horizon Traders Pvt. Ltd., a company incorporated under the Companies Act 2013 and engaged in the business of wholesale trading of electronic goods, states that the accused Mr. Rajesh Kumar Sharma had purchased goods worth Rs. 12,50,000/- (Rupees Twelve Lakhs Fifty Thousand Only) from the complainant on credit basis in September 2022.

In discharge of the said legally enforceable debt, the accused issued a cheque bearing No. 004521 dated 15.01.2023 for the amount of Rs. 12,50,000/- drawn on Axis Bank Ltd., Andheri Branch, Mumbai, in favour of the complainant.

When the complainant presented the said cheque for encashment on 18.01.2023, the same was returned unpaid by the bank with the endorsement "Funds Insufficient" vide bank memo dated 18.01.2023.

The complainant thereafter sent a legal notice dated 28.01.2023 to the accused via registered post demanding payment of the cheque amount within 15 days of receipt of the notice. The said notice was duly served upon the accused on 01.02.2023. Despite service of the notice, the accused failed and neglected to make payment of the cheque amount within the stipulated period of 15 days.

The accused has thus committed an offence punishable under Section 138 read with Section 142 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881. The accused is liable to be punished with imprisonment for a term which may be extended to two years or a fine which may extend to twice the amount of the cheque, or both.

The complainant seeks that this Honourable Court be pleased to take cognizance of the offence and issue process against the accused, and upon trial convict and sentence the accused appropriately under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881.
